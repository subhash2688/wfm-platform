## Research Limitations & Caveats

- **Title currency:** Corporate roles change frequently. Titles were verified as of the most recent available source but may have changed. Always verify on LinkedIn before outreach.
- **Email addresses:** Only emails found on official company/foundation pages or press releases are included. No emails from data scraping services (RocketReach, ZoomInfo, etc.) are included as these may be inaccurate or outdated.
- **Genentech gap:** The departure of Alexandra Horton (former Foundation Program Manager) leaves a gap. The current program officer for community giving is unknown. A LinkedIn search for "Genentech Foundation" + current employees would help identify the replacement.
- **Google.org opacity:** Google.org does not publish individual contacts or a general inquiry email. This is by design -- they operate primarily through open calls and invited partnerships.
- **Netflix misalignment:** Netflix's philanthropic focus (creative equity in entertainment) does not align with food security or education. The company also does not accept external donation requests. This should be deprioritized.
- **Salesforce invitation-only:** Salesforce Foundation's strategic grants are invitation-only. Cold outreach to the grants team will not work. Focus on employee-driven giving pathways instead.
