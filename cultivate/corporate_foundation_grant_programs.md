## 1. CISCO FOUNDATION

### Program Name
**Global Impact Cash Grant Program**

### What They Fund
Four investment areas:
1. **Crisis Response** - "Mobilizing to provide essentials to communities in need and those in crisis, including food, housing, secure connectivity, and disaster relief" -- THIS IS THE BEST FIT FOR WFM
2. **Education** - Supporting students, teachers, and schools through engagement and skill development
3. **Economic Empowerment** - Building careers, entrepreneurship, and financial access
4. **Climate Resilience** - Clean energy, conservation, regenerative agriculture

Programs must demonstrate innovative technology integration in their approach.

### Eligibility
- Must be IRS 501(c)(3) public charity
- Target audience must be 65%+ economically underserved relative to local area standards
- Maximum overhead: 25% of budget (exceptions considered case-by-case)
- Board chair or officer must review and approve the grant proposal

### Who Cannot Apply
- Hospitals, schools (K-12), academic institutions, universities
- Religious organizations (for religious activities)
- No funding for: general operating costs, events, fundraising, capital building, scholarships, research, membership-based activities

### Grant Amounts
- **First-time recipients: Maximum $75,000**
- Returning grantees: up to $250,000
- Multi-year grants possible for returning grantees

### Application Process
1. Register on Cisco's online grant platform
2. Submit a Letter of Inquiry (LOI) including: organizational details, program description, target population, budget, anticipated impact
3. LOIs reviewed within approximately one business quarter (3 months)
4. Organizations deemed a strong fit are invited to submit a full proposal
5. Electronic donation agreement required upon approval

### Deadlines / Cycles
**CURRENTLY PAUSED** - Nonprofit grant submissions are suspended during a platform transition. Expected to reopen in **early 2026**. Check back at the application page.

### Application URL
https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/about/purpose/social-impact/investments/global-impact-cash-grants.html

### WFM Fit Notes
- Crisis Response pillar explicitly includes food as an essential
- The 65% economically underserved requirement aligns well with community college student demographics
- **IMPORTANT LIMITATION**: Schools and academic institutions are ineligible. WFM must apply as a standalone 501(c)(3) nonprofit, NOT through the colleges
- Technology integration requirement: WFM should emphasize any tech used in pantry operations (inventory systems, scheduling apps, data tracking)

- [Cisco Grant Giving Policies](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/about/purpose/social-impact/investments/grant-giving-policies.html)

## 2. SALESFORCE FOUNDATION / SALESFORCE.ORG

### Program Name
**Salesforce Foundation Strategic Grants** (cash grants)
**Power of Us Program** (product donations)

### What They Fund
Three strategic grantmaking pillars:
1. **STEM and AI Access** - Empowering students with opportunities to build STEM and AI skills and literacy
2. **Educator Empowerment** - Attracting, retaining, and growing quality educators in public schools
3. **College and Career Readiness** - Connecting education to careers through mentorship and aligned learning

Also supports housing/homelessness, climate change, and community development in some capacity.

### Eligibility
- Nonprofit status required
- Must demonstrate alignment with Salesforce's education strategy
- Demonstrated program impact or evidence-based models
- Commitment to measure, learn, and adapt
- Strong, diverse leadership reflective of community served
- Must be in regions with major Salesforce hubs (San Francisco qualifies)

### Who Cannot Apply
- No unsolicited applications accepted
- Does not fund: events, sponsorships, galas, individual scholarships, endowment funding, lobbying

### Grant Amounts
- Not publicly disclosed
- Annual Form 990-PF filings available for FY2022-2024 that detail grants awarded

### Application Process
**INVITATION-ONLY.** Strategic grant proposals accepted exclusively by invitation. The foundation reviews letters of inquiry quarterly but does not accept unsolicited applications. The most viable path is networking with Salesforce employees who can refer your organization internally.

### Deadlines / Cycles
- Quarterly letter of inquiry reviews (by invitation)
- No public application windows

### Application URL
https://www.salesforce.com/company/philanthropy/salesforce-foundation/

### Alternative: Power of Us Program
- 10 free Salesforce subscriptions for eligible nonprofits
- Deep discounts on additional products/services
- Apply through: https://www.salesforce.com/company/power-of-us/

### WFM Fit Notes
- Food security is not a stated focus area
- The "College and Career Readiness" pillar has the closest alignment, but it's focused on K-12 to college pathways, not on supporting students already in college
- **Best path**: Apply for Power of Us (free Salesforce CRM) and build a relationship with Salesforce employees through volunteering at WFM pantries. An employee champion could facilitate an invitation to the grant program
- Salesforce has 70,000+ employees in SF; employee matching gifts and volunteerism are the more realistic entry points

## 3. WELLS FARGO FOUNDATION

### Program Name
**Wells Fargo Community Giving Program**

### What They Fund
Three strategic funding priorities:
1. **Financial Opportunity** - Programs that help people manage money, build savings, improve credit
2. **Housing Access and Affordability** - Affordable housing initiatives, homeownership, homelessness prevention
3. **Small Business Growth** - Small business support, entrepreneurship, economic development

May also fund disaster relief and local community needs on a limited basis.

### Eligibility
- Must hold 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, OR be a qualified governmental agency, tribal entity, or public school/school system
- Must demonstrate fiscal and administrative stability
- Must deliver services addressing critical community needs
- Must be able to measure and report outcomes-based success metrics
- Programs should promote innovative, scalable solutions
- Should incorporate volunteer opportunities for Wells Fargo employees

### Who Cannot Apply
- Individuals
- Political entities or labor/fraternal organizations
- Sports programs
- For-profit companies
- Travel-related requests

### Grant Amounts
- Not publicly disclosed
- Applications must be submitted at least 90 days before any event
- Payment typically arrives within 4 months after approval

### Application Process
1. **Primarily invitation-only** - Eligible organizations receive an invitation code
2. **For uninvited organizations**: Submit a Grant Interest Form (not guaranteed to lead to invitation)
3. If invited, use the invitation code to access the portal at wellsfargo.versaic.com/login/
4. Complete the online application with all required fields and documentation
5. Do not follow up on status for 120 days after submission
6. Declined proposals cannot be resubmitted in the same calendar year

### Deadlines / Cycles
- Rolling/ongoing (invitation-based)
- Event-related requests: at least 90 days before the event

### Application URL
- Portal: https://wellsfargo.versaic.com/login/ (requires invitation code)
- Grant process info: https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/responsibility-and-impact/community-giving/grant-process/
- Contact: wffound@wellsfargo.com

### WFM Fit Notes
- **Food security is NOT a stated funding priority.** The three pillars (financial opportunity, housing, small business) do not directly align with WFM's mission
- However, Wells Fargo does fund broadly in "local community needs" on occasion
- The Grant Interest Form is worth submitting but expectations should be low
- **Better approach**: Wells Fargo has strong employee volunteer programs. Focus on getting Wells Fargo employee volunteer teams at WFM pantry events, which could lead to internal advocacy for a grant
- Wells Fargo also manages private foundations for clients; some of these may fund food security
