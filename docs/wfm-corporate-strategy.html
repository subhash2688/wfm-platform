Fundamentals

Four Ways Companies Give Money

Corporate philanthropy flows through distinct channels — each with different processes, timelines, and effort levels. We should pursue all four.

🏛️

Corporate Foundations

Separate legal entities with formal grant cycles. Published RFPs, structured applications, dedicated program officers. Think Google.org, Cisco Foundation, Wells Fargo Foundation.

$5K – $100K+ · 3–6 month cycle
🤝

CSR Budgets

Internal budgets managed by marketing, HR, or community teams. More flexible, relationship-driven. Faster decisions, but harder to discover — not always listed publicly.

$1K – $50K · 2–12 week turnaround
💰

Employee Matching

Companies match donations their employees make, usually dollar-for-dollar. Apple, Google, Microsoft, Salesforce are among the most generous. Managed via Benevity, YourCause, etc.

$100 – $25K per person · Ongoing
📦

In-Kind Giving

Food products, software licenses, volunteer days, pro-bono services. Not cash, but offsets operating costs and often deepens relationships that lead to cash gifts later.

Variable · 1–4 weeks

Key insight: Start with corporate foundations (structured, discoverable) and CSR budgets (relationship-driven, faster). Layer in matching gifts and in-kind as relationships deepen.

Claude Code: Your Research Agent

Here's exactly how to use Claude Code to automate the most time-consuming parts of corporate prospecting. No paid tools required to start.

Workflow 1: Company Research Pipeline
1

Start with a seed list of Bay Area companies

Give Claude Code your target list (from the tracker we built) and ask it to research each company's CSR/giving program. It visits their website, finds the community or foundation page, and extracts focus areas, grant sizes, and application info.

2

Pull 990 data for foundations

Claude Code can query ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer API to pull Form 990 data — showing who each foundation has funded, grant amounts, and focus areas. This tells you if they've funded similar orgs.

ProPublica API990 analysis
3

Score and prioritize automatically

Based on the research, Claude Code populates your prospect tracker with alignment scores, proximity scores, and capacity scores — giving you a ranked list of who to approach first.

spreadsheet generationscoring logic
4

Generate personalized outreach drafts

For your top 15 prospects, Claude Code drafts individualized outreach emails referencing each company's specific CSR priorities, geographic focus, and what they've funded before. You review, edit, and send.

email draftingpersonalization
Workflow 2: Grant Application Support

📝 Proposal Drafting

  • Feed Claude Code the RFP/application questions + your org data
  • It generates first-draft narrative sections
  • Pulls in relevant impact data and talking points
  • You refine the voice and add personal stories

📊 Impact Report Generation

  • Feed it your raw program data (students served, meals distributed)
  • Generates polished quarterly impact reports
  • Creates funder-specific versions highlighting what each cares about
  • Outputs as Word docs or PDFs ready to send

How to get started: Install Claude Code from your terminal (npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code), then point it at your prospect tracker and say: "Research the top 10 companies in this spreadsheet — find their CSR/giving programs, pull 990 data if they have a foundation, score them, and draft outreach emails." It handles the rest.

Execution

90-Day Launch Plan

A phased approach that starts with quick wins and scales up. Designed for someone doing this alongside other responsibilities.

1
Weeks 1–2: Build the Foundation

Research & Setup

Gather impact data from all three campuses. Set up Claude Code. Run the research pipeline on 30–50 Bay Area companies. Build your scored prospect list. Sign up for Instrumentl trial to discover open grant opportunities.

2
Weeks 3–4: First Contact

Outreach Launch

Send personalized outreach to your top 15 prospects (Claude Code drafts, you edit and send). Submit applications for any open foundation grants with near-term deadlines. Follow up after 5–7 days on non-responses.

3
Weeks 5–8: Deepen Relationships

Meetings & Proposals

Conduct discovery meetings with interested companies. Use Claude Code to draft tailored proposals based on meeting notes. Invite contacts to campus visits or food distribution events. Continue outreach to next batch of prospects.

4
Weeks 9–12: Close & Scale

Secure First Commitments

Follow up on pending proposals. Secure first corporate gifts. Begin stewardship (thank-yous, impact updates). Evaluate your pipeline conversion rates. Expand prospect list and begin cycle 2.
